# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indian exporters and e-commerce sellers who need reliable international courier and freight services. Business owners shipping B2B cargo. Consumers sending personal shipments abroad. Primary context: evaluating reliability and getting a fast rate estimate before committing. Job: ship internationally without anxiety about customs, delays, or lost parcels.

## Product Purpose

Bombino Express is India's international courier service since 1995. The site's job is to convert a visitor's logistical anxiety into confident action — either requesting a quote, tracking a shipment, or calling a sales rep. Success = rate requested or contact form submitted.

## Brand Personality

Established authority. Calm precision. Global reach.

Three words: reliable, authoritative, precise.

The brand should feel like the logistics partner you trust with your most important shipment — not flashy, not startup-casual. Premium in the way an airport's premium lounge feels premium: quiet confidence, not showmanship.

## Anti-references

- Generic courier sites (FedEx clone aesthetics, stock-photo-heavy with orange gradients)
- Startup-casual UI with excessive round-cornered cards and gradient CTAs
- Heavy parallax/scroll-jacking that slows perceived performance
- Dark-background neon/glow tech aesthetics

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence over decoration** — every visual choice should communicate reliability and scale, not aesthetic novelty for its own sake.
2. **Urgency without anxiety** — the visitor is already stressed about their shipment; reduce friction, not add dazzle.
3. **Show, don't list** — replace icon grids and bullet lists with spatial hierarchy, editorial layout, and narrative structure.
4. **Motion earns its place** — animations reveal content and guide attention; they don't perform for their own sake.
5. **Global without generic** — the brand serves 150+ countries but is proudly Indian in origin; the design should feel internationally premium, not interchangeably SaaS.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Reduced-motion respected (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce). Adequate color contrast on all text. Screen-reader-friendly landmarks and labels. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile.
